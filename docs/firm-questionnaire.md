# Questions for the Accounting Firm — Before Development Continues

Thank you for working with us on the accounting platform. We have finished the planning stage. Before we start building the system's foundation (logins, user roles, client data separation and audit history), we need your answers to the questions in **Part A**.

The questions in **Part B** are not urgent. We will need them in later stages, so please answer them when you can.

Please write your answers under each question. If you are unsure, write "not sure" and we will discuss it.

---

## PART A — Needed before we continue

### A1. Approval of the plan
1. Have you reviewed the planning documents we sent? Do you approve them, or do you have corrections?

### A2. Your team and their access
2. What roles does your team have? We have proposed the five below. Please correct, add or remove.
   - **Admin:** manages user accounts and settings
   - **Partner / Manager:** final approver for all clients
   - **Reviewer:** reviews and approves work for assigned clients
   - **Accountant:** prepares entries, returns and reports
   - **Bookkeeper:** encodes transactions and uploads documents
3. Should staff see **only the clients assigned to them**, or all clients?
4. Should a staff member be **blocked from approving their own work** (one person prepares, a different person approves)? *We recommend yes.*
5. Should the Admin be able to see clients' financial data, or only manage user accounts? *We recommend user accounts only.*
6. About how many staff will use the system at launch?

### A3. Your clients' access (business owners)
7. Will your clients log in to the system themselves, for example to upload invoices, answer your questions, or view reports?
8. If yes, what access should they have? We have proposed three roles:
   - **Owner:** sees everything for their business and approves returns
   - **Staff uploader:** uploads documents and answers requests only
   - **Viewer:** read-only access to approved reports
9. About how many clients (businesses) will you put on the system at launch?

### A4. Login security
10. All your staff will be required to use **two-step login** (a code from a phone app such as Google Authenticator). Is that acceptable?
11. For your clients, should two-step login be: required for everyone / required only for the owner who approves returns / optional?

### A5. Who runs the system, and where data is stored
12. After launch, who will run and maintain the system?
   - (a) We host and maintain it, and your firm subscribes
   - (b) It is handed over and your firm hosts and maintains it
   - (c) Not decided yet
13. The system will likely store data on secure cloud servers **outside the Philippines** (for example, Singapore). Is this acceptable, subject to your Data Privacy Act review?
14. Who is your firm's **Data Protection Officer**, if any?

## PART B — Needed later (please answer when you can)

### B1. Tax rules and forms
15. Who is the **CPA or tax lead** who will review and approve the tax rules and form computations in the system? Can a **second person** also approve?
16. Please send your **list of BIR forms** you currently prepare for clients.
17. For each form type you prepare, can you provide **sample computations** (with fictional or anonymized numbers) so we can test the system against your expected results?

### B2. BIR registration of the system
18. Under BIR rules (RMO No. 9-2021), software that records sales and purchases or produces BIR reports may need to be **registered by each client with their RDO**. We have not assumed the system is exempt. Will your firm consult **tax counsel** or ask the **BIR directly** to confirm? We have prepared six specific questions for that.
19. Are your staff **accredited by the BIR as tax agents**?

### B3. How you work today
20. Briefly describe your monthly process: how documents come in, who reviews, and who files.
21. How do you file returns: **eFPS, eBIRForms**, or both?
22. Must the business owner **approve every return** before you file it?
23. If an error is found in a month that is already closed, do you prefer to:
   - (a) correct it in the current month, or
   - (b) reopen the closed month (manager approval only)?
24. Must journal entry numbers be **continuous with no gaps** each year?
25. How long do you keep client records today?

### B4. Volume and budget
26. On average, how many documents (invoices, receipts and similar) per client per month?
27. Do you have a monthly budget in mind for hosting and running the system?

### B5. Your clients
28. We have a separate questionnaire for your clients (business registration, books of accounts, invoicing and taxes). Could you send it to **one or two pilot clients** first?

# Review of the Firm's Client Workbooks (one client, September 2026)

**Date:** 2026-10-10. **Source:** four Excel workbooks for one of the Firm's clients: books of accounts, two semi-monthly payrolls, and a petty cash summary. The workbooks are **not** stored in the repository (`*.xlsx` is git-ignored). This summary contains **no client names, TINs or salaries**.

**Purpose:** to understand how the Firm actually works, so that the platform mirrors it. Statements about tax treatment below describe **what the spreadsheets do**, not what the rules are. Every item marked ❓ needs the Firm's CPA.

## 1. How the workbooks work

### Books of accounts (VAT-registered trading business, Q2 2026)

Staff type each source document into two "process first" sheets. Every other sheet is derived from them by formulas.

| Sheet | Typed by staff | Computed |
|---|---|---|
| Cash Disbursement Journal (CDJ) | Date, supplier TIN, receipt no. (SI/OR/CI), amount paid | Supplier name and address are looked up from a supplier list by TIN. Input VAT = amount ÷ 1.12 × 0.12. The expense column = amount ÷ 1.12. A cross-foot check column must be 0. |
| CDJ, non-VAT suppliers | The same | The full amount goes to expense; no input VAT |
| Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ) | Date, customer, TIN, sales invoice no., amount | Totals. An EWT (tax withheld by the customer) column exists but is empty. |
| Sales Journal | Nothing (copies the CRJ) | Net sales = gross ÷ 1.12; output VAT = gross − net |
| Purchases Journal | Nothing (copies both CDJs) | Splits into VAT goods, VAT services and non-VAT; input VAT; invoice total |
| Supplier list | Name, TIN, address, default expense category | Used by the CDJ lookups |
| Quarterly summary | — | Gross sales, VAT sales, output VAT, purchases by type, input VAT, VAT payable (output − input) |

### Payroll (semi-monthly)

- **Basic pay** = monthly ÷ 2. **Daily rate** = monthly ÷ 26. **Hourly rate** = daily ÷ 8.
- **Absences and lateness** are deducted at the daily or hourly rate.
- **Overtime** = hourly × 1.25 or × 1.3, taken from a separate overtime sheet.
- **Holidays:** a regular holiday adds one day's pay; a special day adds 30%.
- **First half of the month:** no deductions.
- **Second half of the month:** deducts SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions, SSS and Pag-IBIG loans (including calamity loans) and cash advances, and records employer shares.
- **Contribution amounts are typed in by hand;** there is no table.
- **Monthly remittance totals:** employee share + employer share for each agency.
- **Payslips** for each employee are generated by formulas.

### Petty cash (imprest)

- The fund is ₱40,000.
- In each period (about weekly), receipts are listed and assigned to expense columns.
- A check for exactly the amount spent replenishes the fund, so cash on hand + the check = ₱40,000 every time.

## 2. Errors found in the workbooks

| # | Finding | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Purchases Journal copies only 9 of the 15 non-VAT CDJ rows | Non-VAT purchases are understated by ₱5,307 (₱14,930 instead of ₱20,237). VAT payable is unaffected. |
| 2 | The general-ledger debit total starts one row too low | It omits the first row (₱1,055): ₱887,142.68 instead of ₱888,197.68 |
| 3 | Typed-over formulas (e.g. `=15805*38`, `=43102.01-8108`) | There is no record of why the figures were changed |
| 4 | Labels left over from older periods ("FEBRUARY - 2022", "SEPTEMBER" next to April dates) | Risk of filing with the wrong period |
| 5 | A payroll gross-pay formula refers to an empty header cell | Harmless today; wrong if that cell is ever filled |
| 6 | The expense category comes from the supplier list (e.g. a gas station set to repairs) | One wrong entry in the list misclassifies every purchase from that supplier |
| 7 | Floating-point noise (e.g. `64019.99999999999`) | Rounding differences in totals |

**How the platform prevents these:** totals come from the ledger, never from copied ranges (1, 2). Posted entries are immutable and corrections are reversals with a reason (3). Each entry belongs to a dated period (4). Amounts are exact decimals (7).

## 3. Questions for the Firm's CPA ❓

1. **Sales are recorded when cash is collected** (the Sales Journal copies the CRJ; the A/R columns are empty). Is that the intended basis for output VAT?
2. **No tax is withheld on supplier payments**, including rent and retainer fees. Is this client not required to withhold on these?
3. **There is no withholding-tax column on compensation in payroll.** Are all employees below the taxable threshold, or is tax computed elsewhere?
4. **The SSS employer-share column is empty**, while PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG employer shares are filled in. Is that deliberate?
5. **Contribution amounts, overtime multipliers and holiday rates:** which official tables and versions does the Firm use?
6. **Do these workbooks cover one business (one TIN) or two?** The sales books and the payroll/petty cash appear to belong to different lines of business.
7. **Does the Firm expense goods to Purchases (periodic method) or record inventory (perpetual)?**
